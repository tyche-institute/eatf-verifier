# EATF v0.6.1 — portable conference download

This patch makes the public ML-DSA workshop ZIP directly replayable from its
extracted directory after the one-time verifier setup.

## Changes

- include the Python cross-language runner and shell launcher in the ZIP;
- locate the prepared verifier checkout through `EATF_REPO_ROOT` or `PATH`;
- correct download-specific setup, checksum, and replay commands;
- keep the six prepared packages and their expected outcomes unchanged; and
- extract and replay the built ZIP in CI, including every listed SHA-256 check.

The replay still produces 24 observations: six packages, two policies, and two
independent implementations, with zero verdict or first-failure-code
mismatches.
